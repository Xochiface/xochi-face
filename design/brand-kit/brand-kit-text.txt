VOICE · IN HER OWN WORDS

I'm so excited to be sharing this experience with others. To me, this work is not just about supporting the lymphatic
system or sculpting the face. There's also an exchange that happens deeper than the skin.
Our face holds more tension than we realize -- and many people don't notice it until they feel it begin to release.
Sometimes the glow starts with releasing what you've been carrying.

VOICE PRINCIPLES                                                             02 · PACING

six rules for the table.                                                     Short sentences. Periods land like exhales. Em-dashes for breath.

01 · PERSON                                                                  04 · GARDEN VOCABULARY

Default to "you" and "your". Nicole speaks to the client, not at them.       Tend. Bloom. Root. Season. Cycle. Used as logic -- never as costume.

03 · SPECIFICITY                                                             06 · NO

Name body parts plainly -- lips, eyes, forehead. Specificity is the warmth.  No emoji. No exclamation points. No "unlock", "boost", or "transform".

05 · HUMILITY

"It might sound a little crazy." Undersell the spiritual. Never lecture.

           THE MARK                                                             07 / 27

   the primary lockup.

              The full lockup is the brand's first impression -- portrait,
              wordmark, and the line "lymphatic facials" stacked together.
              Reach for it on covers, signage, and printed pieces -- anywhere
              there's room to breathe.
              Minimum height is 80px digital / 1 in print. Below that, use the
              icon or wordmark alone.
              Clear space around the lockup equals one full "x"-height of the
              wordmark.
